# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming experience for everyone, whatever their background or experience. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open and respectful.

## Our standards

Things that help: being kind and patient, welcoming questions, giving and taking feedback with good faith, and keeping the focus on the work.

Things that do not belong here: personal attacks, harassment of any kind, sharing others' private information, and conduct that would reasonably be seen as unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these standards, please raise it with the maintainer through an issue or a private channel. Reports will be handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, and contributions that do not follow this code, and may take further action where needed.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
